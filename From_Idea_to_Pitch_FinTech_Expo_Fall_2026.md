# From Idea to Pitch: Workflow for FinTech/Automation Projects

**FinTech Expo Fall 2026 — Student Guide**

## Workflow (8 Steps)

1. **Identify the Problem** — Pick a real pain point in taxation, finance, accounting, audit, or risk (from your own experience, a business, or research).
2. **Research & Validate** — Study existing solutions, literature, and talk to 2–3 potential users to confirm the problem is real and worth solving.
3. **Ideate the Solution** — Decide the format: research paper, automation tool, or FinTech app. Sketch how it works end-to-end.
4. **Design the Solution** — Map the process flow / user journey; list features, data needed, and tools/tech stack (Excel macros, Python, app builder, etc.).
5. **Build a Prototype/MVP** — Create a working demo, mockup, spreadsheet model, or app screen — doesn’t need to be fully functional, just demonstrable.
6. **Test & Refine** — Get feedback from a peer or teacher; fix gaps; check accuracy of calculations/logic.
7. **Financial & Feasibility Check** — Estimate cost to build, potential users/market size, and revenue or savings it creates.
8. **Build the Pitch** — Create a short deck (Problem → Solution → Demo → Market → Financials → Ask) and rehearse a 5–7 minute pitch + Q&A.

## 20-Mark Evaluation Rubric

| # | Component | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Problem Definition & Relevance to theme | 3 |
| 2 | Research/Market Evidence (data, literature, user validation) | 3 |
| 3 | Innovation & Use of Technology/Automation | 4 |
| 4 | Feasibility (technical + financial viability) | 3 |
| 5 | Prototype/Demo Quality | 3 |
| 6 | Pitch Delivery & Presentation | 2 |
| 7 | Q&A Handling | 2 |
| | **Total** | **20** |

> **Tip for students:** Judges weight Innovation (4) and Research/Feasibility (6 combined) the highest — spend the most prep time there, not just on slide design.
